# Telepathy team SOP

## 1. Share context once

Use Telepathy when a decision, change, question, ask, or announcement should survive a meeting or private chat. Do not copy the same update across temporary channels.

Every useful post answers:

1. What changed?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What do you need from the team?

## 2. Choose the right post type

- **Update:** progress or context with no decision required.
- **Decision:** the choice, rationale, owner, and consequence.
- **Question:** a concrete unknown with the person who can move it forward.
- **Announcement:** context everyone should receive.

## 3. Use async by default

Post durable context first. Move to an active conversation when the decision is urgent, ambiguous, or emotionally nuanced. Return the outcome to the original post so the record remains complete.

Temporary channels may host live discussion, but they are not the source of truth.

## 4. Keep responsibility human

Mention someone only when they need to act. Assign an owner for an ask or open question. The owner may delegate execution to agents, but the human owner remains visible and accountable.

## 5. Acknowledge without noise

Use Acknowledge when important context has landed and no reply is required. Reply when you add information, disagree, or need clarification.

## 6. Resolve explicitly

Close a question or ask only when the outcome is written down. Link the accepted artifact or state that no change was required. Do not infer completion from an agent stopping or a temporary channel becoming quiet.

## 7. Record product changes

Every shipped change gets a dated changelog entry with one category:

- Added
- Improved
- Fixed

Describe the impact on people, link the reviewed revision, and avoid announcing planned work as shipped.

## 8. Manage external conversations through review

Telepathy may prepare a draft from shared context. A named human reviews the recipient, evidence, privacy boundary, and wording before anything is sent. Record sent state only from verified delivery evidence.

## 9. Onboard a teammate

1. Send a personal workspace invitation.
2. Verify the invitation was accepted.
3. Select deliberate repository access separately.
4. Complete role, responsibility, timezone, and notification preference.
5. Read and acknowledge **Telepathy starts here**.
6. Publish the first update.

Invited is not onboarded. Repository visibility is not write permission.

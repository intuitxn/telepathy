# Writing as intuitxn

Start with the reader and the thing they need to understand. State the point early, use concrete language, and make claims traceable to evidence.

## Choose the form

| Form | What it should do |
| --- | --- |
| Report | Explain findings, methods, evidence and limits |
| Announcement | Say what is available now, who it helps and what to do next |
| Blog | Develop one useful idea with a clear argument and examples |
| Proposal | Explain a problem, proposed work, scope and the decision needed |
| Other writing | State its purpose, audience and intended destination |

## Draft, review, share

1. Write a self-contained draft. Keep private discussion, raw agent transcripts, credentials and review notes outside its public body.
2. Check each consequential claim. Cite the original source. Distinguish findings from hypotheses and shipped work from plans.
3. Ask the accountable human to review the exact version, intended audience and destination. Changes after review need a new review.
4. Export the reviewed artifact. Export means the files are ready to share; it does not mean they have been published.
5. When publication is authorized, publish to the chosen destination and return the real link to the original thread.

Company ownership does not invent authorship. Name human authors or reviewers accurately; do not present an agent's signed message as a person's message.

Avoid unsupported promises, invented numbers and generic promotional language. Prefer a short, useful piece over a long one that repeats itself.

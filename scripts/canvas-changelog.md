# Changelog

One post per change, dated, with one category. Drafted by Steward; a human approves the send.

## Post format

- **Header:** `## <Category>: <title>` with the date.
- **Categories:** `Added`, `Improved`, `Fixed` — planned work is never listed as shipped.
- **Body:** what changed, why it matters, who it affects.
- **Link:** the exact revision or release it shipped with.

The canonical changelog is `CHANGELOG.md` in the repo; posts here are the team-visible projection.
